A no-derivatives licence would protect the author's identity, but at the cost of making the module un-adaptable — no translations, no local versions, no district-specific adaptations. That would defeat the entire purpose of building it. ShareAlike plus an identity carve-out gets both: anyone may adapt and redistribute it, and nobody may attach someone else's name to clinical content they did not write and cannot vouch for.

This matters specifically because the module issues a certificate. A certificate is an attestation, and an attestation must be traceable to whoever is actually making it.
